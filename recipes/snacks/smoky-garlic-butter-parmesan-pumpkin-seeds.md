# Halcyon's Hearth — Smoky Garlic Butter Parmesan Pumpkin Seeds

**Status:** First-taste success — October 30, 2026. The first two seeds tasted **"awesome"**. An additional 5-minute roast was chosen for extra crunch; the final fully cooled texture has not yet been reported.

Freshly carved Halloween pumpkin seeds, roasted in garlic butter with onion powder, smoked paprika, a pinch of cayenne, and a finishing coat of toasted Parmesan. Savory, smoky, cheesy, and just a little warm.

## Yield, Equipment & Timing

- **Yield:** Approximately 1–1½ cups seeds, depending on the pumpkin.
- **Oven:** 325°F (163°C).
- **Approximate roasting time:** 25–40 minutes total, depending on seed size, moisture, and oven.
- **Prep:** Rinse, dry thoroughly, and ideally air-dry another 10–15 minutes.
- **Cooling:** 10 minutes before the crunch test.
- **Equipment:** Colander, clean kitchen towels, mixing bowl, baking sheet, parchment paper (optional), spatula.

## Ingredients

- 1–1½ cups fresh pumpkin seeds, rinsed and cleaned
- 1½ tbsp butter, melted
- 2 tbsp finely grated Parmesan cheese, divided if desired
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp onion powder
- ¼ tsp smoked paprika
- ¼ tsp kosher salt (reduce if using fine table salt; Parmesan also adds salt)
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- **1 pinch cayenne pepper** — added in the actual Halloween batch for a little heat

## Method

1. **Clean:** Separate the seeds from the pumpkin strings. Rinse in a colander, removing the remaining orange bits.
2. **Dry thoroughly:** Pat the seeds dry with a clean towel. If time allows, let them air-dry for another **10–15 minutes**. Drier seeds roast rather than steam.
3. **Preheat:** Set the oven to **325°F**.
4. **Season:** Toss **1–1½ cups cleaned seeds** with **1½ tbsp melted butter**, **½ tsp garlic powder**, **¼ tsp each onion powder, smoked paprika, kosher salt, and black pepper**, plus **a pinch of cayenne**. **Hold back the Parmesan**.
5. **Spread:** Arrange the seasoned seeds in as even a **single layer** as possible on a baking sheet. Separate clumps so they toast evenly.
6. **Roast:** Roast for approximately **20–30 minutes initially**, stirring every **10 minutes**. Begin checking early if the seeds are small. Some larger, wetter seeds will require more time.
7. **Finish with cheese:** When seeds are nearly dry and crisp, toss with **2 tbsp finely grated Parmesan** (or reserve about 1 tsp for sprinkling after baking). Continue roasting **5–8 minutes**, watching closely: cheese should toast lightly, not burn.
8. **Check doneness:** If seeds are still chewy after a short cooling test, return them to the oven in **5-minute increments** at 325°F, watching the Parmesan. Total time may be closer to **30–40 minutes** for plump seeds.
9. **Cool and taste:** Cool approximately **10 minutes** before judging crunch; seeds firm as they cool. Add the reserved dusting of Parmesan if desired.

## Boil or Don't Boil?

**No boiling is required for this batch.** Roasting cleaned and thoroughly dried seeds directly gives a traditional, firmer shell. An optional **10-minute simmer in salted water** before drying and seasoning may make shells somewhat easier to chew, but was **not** part of this cook-along.

## Why It Works

- **Thorough drying + moderate heat:** Encourages even crisping without scorching the spices.
- **Butter, garlic, onion, and smoked paprika:** Build a savory, lightly smoky base.
- **Cayenne:** A subtle background heat, not intended to overpower.
- **Parmesan added toward the end:** Gives nutty, toasted cheese flavor with less risk of burning during the full roast.
- **Cooling before testing:** Helps distinguish truly chewy seeds from hot seeds that have not yet crisped.
