# Halcyon's Poor-Man Hamburger Helper

**Status:** First-cook success — October 13, 2026. Verdict: **"Yo this is awesome."**

A no-tomato-paste rescue dinner that turned frozen hamburger patties, spaghetti, onions, garlic, broth, milk, and sharp cheddar into a creamy, savory skillet pasta. The original plan was McCormick Thick & Zesty spaghetti sauce mix; with no tomato paste or other tomato products available, the packet was set aside and this entirely different dish emerged.

## Yield & Timing

- **Yield:** About 3–4 servings
- **Time:** Approximately 30–40 minutes
- **Equipment:** Large skillet, pasta pot, spatula, whisk or stirring spoon
- **Pasta:** 8 oz dry spaghetti or other noodles
- **Beef:** 3 frozen hamburger patties (roughly ¾–1 lb total, depending on patty size)

**First-cook details explicitly confirmed:** 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar, about 1½ tbsp cream cheese, and Worcestershire sauce were added; the result was enthusiastically approved. The Worcestershire *quantity* was not reported separately; 1 tsp below is the recommended starting amount from the cook-along. Other quantities reflect the guided recipe, not individually verified measurements.

## Ingredients

- 8 oz dry spaghetti or other pasta
- 3 frozen hamburger patties (approximately ¾–1 lb)
- ½ medium onion, finely diced
- 3–4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
- ¾ cup beef broth (or another savory broth)
- ¾ cup milk
- **1 cup shredded sharp cheddar** — first-cook amount
- **About 1½ tbsp cream cheese** — first-cook amount
- **1 tsp Worcestershire sauce** — starting recommendation; first-cook addition confirmed, exact measurement not
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp paprika (optional)
- Salt, to taste
- Up to ½ cup reserved starchy pasta water, as needed

## Method — Amounts Included in Each Step

1. **Brown the burgers:** Set a large skillet over medium heat. Add **3 frozen hamburger patties**; flip as they thaw and brown, then crumble with a spatula. Add **½ finely diced medium onion**, and cook until onions are soft and the beef is browned and fully cooked (**160°F**). Pour off excess grease, keeping roughly **1 tbsp** of flavorful drippings if available.

2. **Cook the noodles:** While beef cooks, boil salted water and cook **8 oz dry spaghetti (or other noodles)** to al dente according to the package directions. Before draining, reserve **½ cup of starchy pasta water**. Drain without rinsing.

3. **Build the garlic-butter base:** Reduce skillet heat to medium-low. Add **2 tbsp butter** and **3–4 minced garlic cloves** to the beef and onions; stir **30–60 seconds** until fragrant. Sprinkle in **1 tbsp all-purpose flour** and stir for **1 minute** to cook away raw flour taste.

4. **Make a savory creamy sauce:** Slowly stir in **¾ cup beef broth**, scraping up the browned bits. Add **¾ cup milk**, **½ tsp black pepper**, **¼ tsp paprika (optional)**, and **1 tsp Worcestershire sauce** (recommended starting quantity). Simmer gently, stirring, for **3–5 minutes**, until lightly thickened.

5. **Melt cream cheese first:** Turn heat to low. Stir in **about 1½ tbsp cream cheese** until fully incorporated and the sauce is smooth. Turn off the heat; let bubbling stop.

6. **Add sharp cheddar off heat:** Add **1 cup shredded sharp cheddar**, a handful at a time, stirring gently until melted. **Do not boil after adding cheddar** — high heat can make it grainy or oily. Taste before deciding whether to add salt.

7. **Toss and finish:** Add the **8 oz cooked pasta** to the skillet. Toss until evenly coated. If the sauce gets too thick, stir in reserved pasta water **1–2 tbsp at a time (up to ½ cup)** until silky. Let rest **about 2 minutes**, then serve hot.

## Why It Works

- **Browned beef + onion + garlic:** Gives the sauce a rich, savory base with no tomatoes required.
- **Broth and Worcestershire:** Add depth, complementing the cheese rather than making the dish sweet.
- **Flour and milk:** Make a light sauce base so the cheese coats the noodles instead of clumping.
- **Sharp cheddar + a little cream cheese:** Strong cheddar flavor with a smoother texture. The first cook used **a full cup of cheddar** and only **about 1½ tablespoons cream cheese**, rather than the earlier proposed ¾ cup cheddar/2 oz cream cheese.
- **Reserved pasta water:** Lets the finished sauce be loosened gradually without watering down its flavor.
