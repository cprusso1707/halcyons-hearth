# Halcyon's Hearth — Cast-Iron Smoky BBQ Chicken Drummies

**Status:** Family-tested keeper — first cooked October 16, 2026. Verdict: **"It was perfect."**

Bone-in chicken drumsticks seared in cast iron, roasted hot, then lacquered with a smoky, tangy BBQ glaze. Inspired by [Smoky BBQ Chicken Loaded Baked Potatoes](smoky-bbq-chicken-loaded-baked-potatoes.md), but this version lets the chicken stand on its own—no potatoes required.

**Important tested change:** The cook **seared the drumsticks on the stovetop first**, then started the oven roast at **450°F for 10 minutes** before reducing to **425°F for the remainder**. That temperature schedule, not the original all-425°F proposal, produced the photographed and enthusiastically approved batch.

## Yield & Equipment

- **Yield:** Approximately 8 drumsticks; roughly 3–4 servings, depending on appetite.
- **Estimated total:** About 50–65 minutes, including prep and rest; actual first-cook elapsed time was not reported.
- **Gear:** Large oven-safe cast-iron skillet, tongs, small bowl, pastry brush, meat thermometer, oven/broiler.
- **Method:** Season → stovetop sear → 450°F initial roast → 425°F roast → glaze near the end → optional brief broil → rest.
- **Note:** Cook in batches if needed. The drumsticks need room to brown rather than steam.

Measurements are repeated inside each step so there is no need to bounce back to an ingredient list.

## 1. Preheat and season the chicken

1. Preheat oven to **450°F**.
2. Pat **8 chicken drumsticks** thoroughly dry with paper towels.
3. In a large bowl, mix **1 tbsp olive oil**, **1 tsp smoked paprika**, **1 tsp garlic powder**, **1 tsp onion powder**, **½ tsp kosher salt**, **½ tsp black pepper**, and optionally **¼ tsp cayenne or red pepper flakes**.
4. Toss **8 drumsticks** until coated evenly. Let sit approximately **5 minutes** while the skillet warms.

## 2. Sear in cast iron

1. Set a large oven-safe cast-iron skillet over **medium to medium-high heat**. Add **1 tbsp neutral cooking oil** (e.g., canola or vegetable).
2. Arrange the seasoned **8 drumsticks** with space between pieces. Work in batches if the pan is too crowded.
3. Sear for **6–8 minutes total**, turning periodically to develop browned spots around several sides. This is for color and flavor; the chicken will **not** yet be cooked through. If the spices threaten to scorch, lower the burner heat.
4. Leave a thin coating of rendered fat and good browned bits in the skillet; if there is excessive pooled grease, carefully spoon some off.

**First-cook confirmation:** The stovetop sear was used, as originally suggested.

## 3. Roast hot, then lower the heat — tested variation

1. Transfer the uncovered, oven-safe skillet to the **450°F oven**. Roast for **10 minutes**.
2. Reduce oven temperature to **425°F** without removing the skillet. Continue roasting approximately **10–15 minutes** to start, turning the drumsticks about halfway through the overall roasting period.
3. Check internal temperature in the thickest section of a drumstick without touching bone. Proceed to glazing when the chicken is **approaching 160–165°F**. If it is not close yet, continue roasting and check again. Oven times are guides, not substitutes for temperature.

**First-cook confirmation:** **450°F for the first 10 oven minutes, then 425°F for the rest**. Exact time spent at 425°F and measured internal temperatures were not separately reported.

## 4. Mix the tangy BBQ glaze

While the chicken roasts, combine in a small bowl:

- **½ cup BBQ sauce**
- **1 tbsp apple cider vinegar**
- **1 tsp Worcestershire sauce**
- **1 tsp brown sugar**, optional if the sauce needs extra sweetness

Stir smooth. Before brushing the chicken, set aside a little **untouched, clean glaze** if you want to brush on extra at serving. Never reuse a brush or sauce that touched undercooked chicken without cooking it thoroughly.

## 5. Glaze and finish roasting

1. When the chicken is approaching **160–165°F**, carefully remove the skillet from the oven. If there is a deep pool of grease, spoon some off, leaving a thin layer.
2. Brush the drumsticks generously with the **BBQ glaze from Step 4**.
3. Return the skillet to the **425°F oven for approximately 8–12 minutes**, turning and brushing with more glaze halfway through.
4. Check for doneness. **165°F** is the food-safe minimum for chicken, but **175–185°F** is the recommended target here for tender drumstick meat. Keep cooking until the desired temperature is reached; timing varies with drumstick size.

## 6. Optional broiler finish and rest

1. Once fully cooked, set the oven to **broil** and position the broiler-safe skillet roughly **6 inches from the heating element**, if your oven and pan allow it.
2. Broil for **1–2 minutes**, watching continuously for a glossy, bubbling glaze with dark caramelized patches. BBQ sauce contains sugar and can burn quickly.
3. Remove and let rest for **5 minutes**. If desired, finish with a small amount of the **clean reserved glaze**.

**Safety:** The cast-iron handle will be extremely hot. Use dry oven mitts and keep the handle clear while plating.

## Why It Works

- **Stovetop sear:** Builds browned, savory skin and renders some fat before roasting.
- **450°F first 10 minutes:** Encourages additional browning; the subsequent **425°F** reduces the risk of scorching over a longer cook.
- **Glaze added late:** Prevents BBQ sauce sugar from burning before the chicken finishes.
- **Apple cider vinegar and Worcestershire:** Balance sweet BBQ sauce with tang and savory depth.
- **Optional very brief broil:** Adds sticky caramelized edges without drying out the chicken when closely watched.
