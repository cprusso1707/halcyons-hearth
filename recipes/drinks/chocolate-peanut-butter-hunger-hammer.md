# Halcyon's Chocolate Peanut Butter Hunger Hammer

**Status:** First-taste success — October 27, 2026. Verdict: **"Well that absolutely slaps."**

A thick, chocolate-peanut-butter protein shake made to satisfy a milkshake craving while delivering substantial protein. Built from Fairlife chocolate milk, plain yogurt, protein powder, and powdered peanut butter. The focus is **satisfaction**, not simply minimizing calories.

## Yield & Timing

- **Yield:** 1 substantial shake
- **Time:** About 5 minutes
- **Equipment:** Blender
- **Texture:** Thick and creamy; adjust with ice or a splash of milk

**First-taste note:** The shake was enthusiastically approved. The recipe below preserves the suggested amounts from the cook-along; the exact amounts used in the successful shake were not separately confirmed.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Fairlife chocolate milk
- ½ cup plain yogurt (regular works; Greek adds more protein and thickness)
- 1 scoop protein powder (chocolate or vanilla)
- 2 tbsp powdered peanut butter
- ½–1 cup ice
- Optional: Tiny pinch of salt to deepen the chocolate and peanut butter flavors

## Method — Amounts Included in Each Step

1. **Start with the liquid:** Pour **1 cup Fairlife chocolate milk** into a blender.
2. **Add the protein and creaminess:** Add **½ cup plain yogurt**, **1 scoop protein powder**, and **2 tbsp powdered peanut butter**.
3. **Chill and thicken:** Add **½–1 cup ice** and, if desired, a **tiny pinch of salt**.
4. **Blend:** Blend **30–45 seconds**, until thick, smooth, and creamy.
5. **Rest and adjust:** Let sit **about 1 minute**. If too thick, blend in a small splash of Fairlife; if too thin, add a little more ice and blend again.

## Optional Satiety Boosters (Not Part of the Confirmed First Taste)

- **1 tbsp chia seeds:** Adds fiber and some fat; let the blended shake sit **10–15 minutes** for the seeds to hydrate and thicken it.
- **¼ cup rolled oats:** Adds fiber and complex carbohydrates; blend thoroughly for a heartier texture.

These add-ins also increase the calories. The goal is a satisfying drink, not a claim that they will suppress appetite for a specific amount of time.

## Estimated Nutrition — Base Shake

**Approximate, not calculated from exact package labels:**

- **Protein:** 40–55 g
- **Calories:** 330–450

Actual nutrition depends on the Fairlife variety, yogurt type, protein powder, and serving size. Optional add-ins are **not** included in the estimate.

## Why It Works

- **Milk + protein powder + yogurt:** Build a protein-forward base.
- **Powdered peanut butter:** Adds peanut-butter flavor without needing regular peanut butter.
- **Yogurt + ice:** Make a creamy milkshake texture.
- **Optional fiber-rich add-ins:** Can make the shake feel more substantial.
