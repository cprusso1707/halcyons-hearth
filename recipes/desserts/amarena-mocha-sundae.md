# Amarena Mocha Sundae

A dangerously good use for leftover chocolate-coffee frosting.

## Ingredients

- Vanilla ice cream
- Chocolate-coffee frosting
- Amarena cherries
- A little Amarena cherry syrup
- Optional: whipped cream
- Optional: toasted chopped hazelnuts, pecans, or almonds
- Optional: tiny pinch of flaky salt

## Quick Mocha Sauce

For each sundae, combine:

- 2 tbsp chocolate-coffee frosting
- 1–2 tsp milk or cream
- 1/2 tsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- Small pinch instant coffee or espresso powder, to taste

Warm gently in the microwave for about 10 seconds at a time, stirring between rounds, until smooth and pourable. Add milk or cream a little at a time rather than all at once.

The extra cocoa and coffee deepen the flavor and stretch the frosting into a less-sweet sauce without losing the richness.

## Assemble

1. Scoop vanilla ice cream into a bowl.
2. Pour the warm mocha sauce over the ice cream.
3. Add 2–3 Amarena cherries, whole or roughly chopped.
4. Drizzle over a small spoonful of the Amarena syrup.
5. Finish with whipped cream and toasted chopped nuts if available.
6. Add a tiny pinch of flaky salt if you want the chocolate and cherry flavors to pop even harder.

## Hearth Note

Chocolate + coffee + Amarena cherry + hazelnut is the preferred full-send version.

The sundae is already sweet from the ice cream and cherries, so a darker, slightly bitter mocha sauce works better here than a heavily sweetened frosting-style topping.
